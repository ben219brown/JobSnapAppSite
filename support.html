JobSnapApp Support & FAQ

Effortless Management for Trade Contractors

General Information

1. What is JobSnap?

JobSnap is a mobile app designed for trade contractors to manage job-site records on the go. Store client contact info, capture site photos, log service notes, track crew hours, and keep everything organized in one secure place.

2. Which devices does JobSnap support?

JobSnap requires Android 8.0 (Oreo / API 26) or higher (optimized for Android 15/16). It is designed for phones and tablets, working offline so you can document job sites even without cell or Wi-Fi coverage.

Account & Setup

3. How do I get started?

1. Install JobSnap from Google Play.

2. Open the app and create a new account (enter a password you’ll remember).

3. Select the trade categories you work in (e.g., Plumbing, HVAC, Carpentry, Electrical).

4. You’re ready to add clients, photos, crew hours, and service details.

4. How do I log in after I’ve signed up?

On the Login screen, enter your password or use Google Sign-In / Biometric login. Once authenticated, you’ll be taken straight into your trade dashboard.

5. Can I change my password?

Yes. Open the navigation drawer menu and tap Change Password. Enter your current password, then type and confirm your new password.

6. Can I customize which trades I track?

Yes—open the navigation drawer and tap Edit Trades. Select or deselect any trade categories that apply to your business, then tap Confirm.

7. How do I enable biometric (fingerprint/face) login?

After your first password sign-in, you’ll see a prompt to enable biometric login if your device supports it. Tap Enable and follow the on-screen dialog. Future logins will then let you use your fingerprint or face instead of typing your password.

Client & Contact Management

8. How do I add a new client?

Select your active trade, tap the ＋ Add Client button at the bottom-right, fill in the client’s name, phone, email, and address details, then tap Save.

9. How can I import existing phone contacts?

On the Client Selection screen, tap the Import Contacts (mini contact icon) next to the ＋ button. Grant contacts permission, select a contact, and JobSnap will automatically pre-fill their name, phone, and email into the client form.

10. Can I copy a client to another trade?

Yes! Long-press any client card on the Client Selection screen and tap Copy to Another Trade. Select from any of your other active trades to instantly duplicate the client and their contact details.

11. How do I contact a client directly from the app?

In the client’s card or detail screen, tap the Call (phone), Email, or Location (map) icons to dial, email, or navigate directly via GPS without leaving JobSnap.

Photos, Notes & App Features

12. How do I capture and view job-site photos?

Capture: In a client’s detail screen, tap the Camera icon to snap a photo. Photos are automatically saved and organized under that client.

Camera Controls: Tap the camera flip icon in the top corner of the camera screen to toggle between front and rear lenses.

View & Zoom: Tap any photo thumbnail to open the full-screen photo viewer with smooth pinch-to-zoom and single-finger panning.

Custom Folders: Tap the Create Folder icon to organize before-and-after photos into subfolders.

13. How do I record service notes?

Within a client’s detail view, tap ＋ Add Note under Service Notes. Each note creates a timestamped card entry that you can edit or delete anytime with confirmation prompts.

14. How do I track crew hours and export payroll reports?

Track Hours: Tap the Crew Hours (clock icon) button on the client detail page, select active crew members, and log daily hours worked.

Export CSV: On the Crew Hours screen, tap Export Payroll CSV Report to generate a formatted CSV file (with daily breakdown, hourly rates, and total payouts) ready to email or open in Excel/Google Sheets.

Search & Date Navigation: Use the search bar to filter crew members, and use the month/week buttons to inspect past pay periods.

15. How do I change photo resolution quality?

Open the navigation drawer, tap Settings → Photo Quality, and choose Low, Medium, or High to balance image detail against storage size.

16. Does JobSnap support dark mode?

Yes—JobSnap includes a dark mode toggle switch directly in the navigation drawer menu.

Data Backup & Security

17. How do Google Drive auto-backups and retention limits work?

Automated Backups: JobSnap automatically backs up your database, photo metadata, custom photo folders, and settings to a secure JobSnap Backups folder in your Google Drive.

Retention Policy: In Settings, choose your auto-backup frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly) and set a Backup Retention Limit (3, 5, 10, 30, or Unlimited). JobSnap automatically prunes older archives so your Drive storage stays clean.

Data Safety: Backups use non-destructive SQLite WAL checkpointing, so your live app data remains 100% active and visible while backing up.

18. Is my data secure?

Absolutely. Credentials and session tokens are encrypted using Android EncryptedSharedPreferences (AES-256), and all network traffic uses TLS 1.2/1.3 (HTTPS) encryption in transit.

Troubleshooting & Support

19. What should I do if I encounter an error?

1. Make sure you’re running the latest version of JobSnap from Google Play.

2. Restart your device.

If the problem persists, email us at jobsnapappinfo@gmail.com with a brief description and screenshots. We will do our best to help you right away.
