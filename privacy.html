Privacy Policy for Brown Family Apps for Android

Last Updated: March 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Brown Family Apps handles information in connection with Brown Family Apps mobile applications or games (the "Service").

Information We Do Not Collect

We understand the importance of your privacy. Brown Family Apps is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, store, use, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) from users of our Service.

This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.
  • Device identifiers (like IMEI, Android ID, Advertising ID).
  • Location data.
  • Usage data or analytics about how you interact with the app.
  • Any data you might enter into the app (if applicable, though for a simple game like Tic-Tac-Toe, this is less likely).
  • Contacts, photos, or other information stored on your device.

Since we do not collect any personal data, we do not have any data to use, share, or sell.

Advertising

Brown Family Apps applications or games do not contain any third-party advertisements.

Permissions

Brown Family Apps applications or games may request certain permissions to function correctly (e.g., if an app had features like saving game state, it might need storage permission). Any permissions requested are solely for the core functionality of the application and are not used to collect any personal data.

Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or a higher age threshold if applicable in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take necessary actions.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the application or on our website (if applicable). You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted.
