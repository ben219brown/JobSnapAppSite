JobSnapApp FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions & User Guide for Trade Contractors

1. Getting Started & Trade Organization

What is JobSnapApp?

JobSnapApp is a mobile photo documentation and crew hours management application built specifically for trade contractors (Plumbing, Electrical, HVAC, Landscaping, Carpentry, General Contracting, Realty, and more). It helps you organize job site photos, track employee hours, and auto-backup data to Google Drive.

How do I organize clients by trade?

During initial setup, select the trade categories you work in. JobSnapApp keeps an isolated client directory for each trade. You can switch trades anytime using the navigation drawer or by selecting Edit Trades in the menu.

Can I copy a client to another trade?

Yes! Long-press any client card on the Client Selection screen and tap Copy to Another Trade. You can choose from any of your other active trades to instantly duplicate the client and their contact details.

2. Google Drive Backups & Retention Policy

How do automated Google Drive backups work?

JobSnapApp uses Android WorkManager to automatically back up your database, photo metadata, custom photo folders, and application settings to a secure JobSnapApp Backups folder in your personal Google Drive account.

How do I configure backup frequency and retention limits?

Open Settings in JobSnapApp:
  • Auto-Backup Frequency: Choose Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or OFF.
  • Backup Retention Limit: Set how many recent backups to keep on Google Drive (3, 5, 10, 30, or Unlimited). JobSnapApp automatically prunes older archives so your storage stays clean.
  • Backup on Wi-Fi Only: Toggle ON to save mobile data.

Will backing up erase or affect my live app data?

No. Backups use non-destructive SQLite WAL (Write-Ahead Logging) checkpointing, so your app data remains 100% active and visible while backing up.

3. Crew Hours & Payroll CSV Export

How do I track employee time and crew hours?

Open any client details page and tap the Crew Hours (clock icon) button. Select active crew members, enter daily hours, and tap Save.

How do I export hours for payroll?

On the Crew Hours screen, tap Export Payroll CSV Report. JobSnapApp generates a CSV file with employee names, phone numbers, hourly pay rates, daily hours breakdown (Sun–Sat), weekly total hours, and calculated payouts ready to email or open in Excel/Google Sheets.

Can I search crew members or navigate past pay periods?

Yes. Use the search bar at the top of the Crew Hours screen to filter crew members by name or phone, and use the « Month / Month » and ‹ Prev Week / Next Week › buttons to inspect past pay periods.

4. Security, Accounts & Support

Is my app data secure?

Yes. All network communications use TLS 1.2/1.3 (HTTPS) encryption in transit, and credentials use Android EncryptedSharedPreferences (AES-256).

How do I sign out safely?

Open the navigation drawer menu and tap Sign Out. Your session token is cleared and password or Google Sign-In authentication is required to log back in.
